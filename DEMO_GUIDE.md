# Demo guide — NA Associates

Use this sequence to test the key end-to-end flows.

## 1. Public website
1. Start at `/`.
2. Open Projects, use the category filters and search.
3. Open a project detail page.
4. Open Contact or Career and submit a demo form.
5. Click Client Portal.

## 2. Client experience
1. Choose **Continue as Client**.
2. On My Projects, open **Rumah Teduh**.
3. Use the left anchor navigation on desktop, or the bottom **Project sections** control on mobile.
4. Inspect published design files, reports, legal documents, Shopping List, billing, and monthly recap.
5. In Billing & Payments, click **Upload payment proof**.
6. Choose an invoice, enter a payment amount, and submit. A new Pending Verification item is created.

## 3. Admin review
1. Sign out to the public page, click Client Portal, then choose **Continue as Admin**.
2. Open Billing & Payments.
3. Choose the project and review the pending proof.
4. Approve an amount. If the approved amount differs from the client's claim, enter a reason.
5. If the payment exceeds the remaining invoice balance, select Credit to next invoice or Refund.
6. Inspect the invoice register and Activity Log.

## 4. Progress and publication
1. Open Field Operations.
2. Choose a project and create a daily log.
3. Fill out the public work details and optional internal attendance/equipment fields.
4. Save as Draft, then publish it from the report list.
5. Return to Client Portal and confirm that only Published reports are visible.

## 5. Shopping and materials
1. Open Shopping & Materials.
2. Add a new material requirement; it starts as Draft.
3. Change status and publish it.
4. Switch back to Client Portal and inspect Shopping List.
5. Record Material Usage separately to see the distinction between planned procurement and actual use.

## 6. Invoice revision and project hold
1. In Billing & Payments, create an invoice or revise an existing unpaid invoice.
2. Add a mandatory reason for the revision. The old invoice stays archived as Cancelled / Replaced.
3. In Field Operations, use **Place on hold** to simulate the project's operational hold.
4. Visit Client Portal: old records and payment upload remain available, while the project displays a hold notice.
5. In Admin, move the project to Ready to Resume, then confirm Resume.

## 7. Reset
Use **Reset demo data** from Client or Admin to restore the starting sample data.
